# Codewire — Outreach Templates (1:1, behind-the-scenes)

Private emails, not public posts. Personalize the first line each time — generic
blasts get ignored (and flagged as spam).

---

## 1. Trade schools / apprenticeship programs (the strongest long-term wedge)

**Who to email:** electrical program instructors, department heads, or
apprenticeship coordinators at community colleges, technical schools, IBEW/JATC
training centers. Find them on the school's program page.

**Why it works:** students who learn on Codewire keep using it on the job — you
get habit-forming adoption before competitors. Free Pro for students costs you
almost nothing and builds goodwill + word of mouth.

**Subject:** Free NEC calculator access for your electrical students

**Body:**
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm the developer of Codewire (codewire.tools) — a free web app that combines
> the five NEC calculators electricians use most: voltage drop, conduit fill,
> conduit bending, box fill, and wire ampacity. It works offline on any phone
> and shows an instant pass/fail to the code.
>
> I'd like to offer **free Pro access to your students and instructors** — no
> cost, no catch. It might be useful for labs and homework where they're sizing
> conductors, checking conduit fill, or laying out bends, and it reinforces the
> NEC sections behind each calculation.
>
> If that's useful, I'm happy to set up a class code or just point your students
> to it. And if there's anything that would make it more helpful for teaching,
> I'd genuinely value your input.
>
> Thanks for what you do training the next round of electricians.
>
> [Your name]
> codewire.tools · codewire.tools@gmail.com

**Follow-up (if no reply after ~1 week):**
> Hi [Name], just following up in case this got buried — happy to give your
> electrical students free Pro access to Codewire (codewire.tools) anytime. No
> rush, and no worries if it's not a fit. Thanks!

## 3. Local electrical supply house (optional, low-effort)

Ask the counter manager if you can leave a small card/QR ("Free NEC calculators
— codewire.tools") by the register. Electricians are standing right there, often
mid-calculation. No email needed — just ask in person or call.

---

## Tips
- Send from the codewire.tools@gmail.com address (or a real name) — consistency
  builds trust.
- One personalized line up top ("Saw your program covers conduit bending in
  lab…") beats a polished generic email.
- Track replies; the schools that say yes are also your best testimonial source.
